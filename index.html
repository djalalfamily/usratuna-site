Our story

From Chad, Sudan and Egypt, to every corner of the world we now call home.

Our family carries names, dates and stories that once passed around one table. Today we are spread across countries, languages and generations, and those threads are easy to lose. We built Usratuna so ours are not: a living family tree, a way to find and reach one another, and a record we keep together and hand to our children.

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَاكُمْ شُعُوبًا وَقَبَائِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوا
